DMCA / Copyright

This page explains NEXTVIDEO's copyright complaint process. The U.S. DMCA may apply only where legally applicable; rights holders may also submit complaints under other applicable copyright laws.

Respect for copyright

NEXTVIDEO respects intellectual property rights. If you believe that material or activity associated with NEXTVIDEO infringes your copyright, you may submit a copyright complaint for review.

What a copyright notice should contain

To help us evaluate a complaint, provide:

  • Your name and contact information.
  • A description identifying the copyrighted work you believe has been infringed.
  • The URL or other information sufficient to identify the material or activity at issue.
  • A statement that you have a good-faith belief that the disputed use is not authorized by the copyright owner, its agent, or applicable law.
  • A statement that the information in the notice is accurate and, where applicable, made under penalty of perjury.
  • Your physical or electronic signature.

Where to send a complaint

Email: copyright@nextvideo.in

Please use the subject line Copyright / DMCA Notice and include the information above.

Review and response

NEXTVIDEO may review properly submitted complaints and may take appropriate action, which can include restricting processing associated with an identified URL or other measures reasonably available to the service.

Good-faith complaints

Copyright notices should be accurate and submitted by the copyright owner or an authorized representative. Knowingly false or materially misleading complaints may have legal consequences.

Counter-notifications

Where a copyright removal or restriction process is subject to an applicable law that provides a counter-notification procedure, an affected user may submit a valid counter-notification through the applicable process. Any counter-notification must contain the information required by the relevant law.

Repeat infringement and abuse

NEXTVIDEO may take reasonable measures against repeated unlawful use or repeated infringement associated with the service. We may also reject abusive, incomplete, fraudulent, or materially misleading complaints.

Important limitation

This page does not claim that NEXTVIDEO is automatically protected by any particular safe-harbor regime. The legal obligations and protections applicable to a service depend on its actual operations, jurisdiction, and compliance with the relevant law.
