What is a Snapchat Video Downloader?

NEXTVIDEO provides an online Snapchat video downloader for supported publicly accessible Snapchat URLs. The page is designed to keep the process straightforward: copy a Snapchat link, paste it into the tool and submit it for processing.

How to Download a Snapchat Video

You can follow this simple workflow on a phone, tablet or computer:

  • Open Snapchat and copy the URL of the supported public video.
  • Paste the Snapchat link into the NEXTVIDEO input box above.
  • Select Download Video and wait for processing to complete.

Download Snapchat Videos Online

Snapchat videos are commonly shared through direct links. A supported public Snapchat video URL can be submitted through the same copy-and-paste workflow.

Snapchat Video Downloader for Android, iPhone and PC

The interface is responsive so the same basic workflow can be used from mobile or desktop browsers. On a phone, copy the Snapchat video link and paste it into your browser. On a computer, copy the Snapchat video URL and paste it directly into the downloader.
